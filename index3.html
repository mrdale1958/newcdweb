From Labels to Portals

Have more to say than fits the label?

A museum caption, a street sign, a trifold poster, a slide show, a shelf tag, a postcard, all those are labels we use to talk to others. But there is just so much you can say on a label. With Cities and Desire, anyone can use AR to expand labels into portals for more: more connection, more experiences, more stories.

How it works

  1. 01Build the scene

    Drag models, pictures, sound, and text onto a canvas, then put the scenes in order.

  2. 02Stick the scene to something physical.

    Choose the anchor: a museum label, a site plan, a poster, a notice board.

  3. 03Change the scene any time

    Rewrite what the scene says whenever you like, and every experience already anchored keeps working.

Agencies and studios

Author activations for your clients

White-label authoring under your own brand, with the client relationship, the contract, and the renewal staying yours. We stay behind it and keep the codes alive.

Visitor and welcome centres

A step up from the trifold

The room already has a budget for a foam model and a folded leaflet, and both go out of date the day the design changes. This one doesn't.

Campuses and trails

One design, every stop

Post the same printed marker at every stop on a trail, campus, or corridor. Each one shows the content for that spot, and you get numbers per stop without building per stop.

Shops and showrooms

The long version, on the shelf

A card by the till, a poster in the window, a tag on the shelf. Change what it says whenever the stock does. The shelf stays a shelf.

An example collaboration

Update infrastructure for a permanent gallery

  1. Audit the exhibition and find where the story has outgrown the labels.
  2. Design and build the first activations with your curators.
  3. Install whatever the AR needs on the floor, and the printed anchors beside it.
  4. Train your staff to author, publish, and update without us.

After that, new scholarship, a new language, a school-holiday version, or a tie-in with the show upstairs is an afternoon's work rather than a budget cycle.

Handover is the point. Success is your curator publishing without emailing us.
